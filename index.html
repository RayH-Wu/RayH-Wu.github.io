  1. ICMA
    Deep Hydro-Sim: Structured Residual Hydrodynamics for the RL-Based AUV Control
    Ruihan Wu, Chunying Li, Qirong Lei, and 3 more authors
    In 2026 IEEE International Conference on Mechatronics and Automation (ICMA), 2026
    Abs Bib PDF Code

    Deep Hydro-Sim bridges a high-fidelity underwater simulator and a fast rigid-body engine by learning residual hydrodynamic forces from Stonefish and injecting them into MuJoCo as a real-time plugin. The result improves open-loop replay fidelity and stabilizes downstream SAC training for AUV control without sacrificing throughput.
