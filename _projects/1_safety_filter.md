Two quadrupeds play a zero-sum interaction on a 5.2 × 3.0 m field. The safety
specification is _coupled_: whether one robot can stay safe depends on what the
other does. Instead of paying for safety with a reward penalty, the rule is
encoded as a least-restrictive filter around the policy, so it reshapes the set
of admissible strategies rather than the score.
