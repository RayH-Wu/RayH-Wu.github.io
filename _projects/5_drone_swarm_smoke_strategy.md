**Sep. 2 - Sep. 6, 2025 | Shenzhen, China**

*National Undergraduate Mathematical Modeling Contest*

## Highlights

- Established a dynamic smoke-diffusion and laser-transmittance model based on the Beer-Lambert law.
- Quantitatively evaluated interference efficiency using the extinction coefficient and aerosol mass-concentration distribution.
- Implemented particle swarm optimization to solve for optimal tactical parameters.
- Maximized the effective screening area and duration against laser-guidance systems.
