**Sep. 2025 - Dec. 2025 | Shenzhen, China**

_Robotics Intelligence and Perception Course Project_

## Highlights

- Integrated a robotic arm with the TurtleBot4 platform for coordinated mobile locomotion and grasping.
- Planned base movement and end-effector operation as a unified motion-control system.
- Used an RGB camera and OpenCV for target recognition and pose estimation.
