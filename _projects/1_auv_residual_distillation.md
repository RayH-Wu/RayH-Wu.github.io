**Dec. 2025 - Mar. 2026 | Philadelphia, PA**

Developed a hybrid simulation workflow for autonomous underwater vehicle control that couples rigid-body dynamics with a learned residual hydrodynamics model.

## Highlights

- Connected Stonefish and MuJoCo to combine high-fidelity underwater simulation with efficient rigid-body simulation.
- Distilled residual hydrodynamic effects into a learned model for use during reinforcement learning control.
- Used the resulting simulation pipeline to support stable AUV control experiments.
