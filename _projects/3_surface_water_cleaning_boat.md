## Highlights

- Designed a vision-based motion-control and target-tracking system.
- Integrated YOLOv8n object detection, PX4, and servo motors.
- Designed robotic-arm gripping mechanisms on both sides of the unmanned vessel.
- Connected the gripping mechanisms with the decision-making system for waste collection.
